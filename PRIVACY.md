# Which Sub? - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Which Sub? is a daily game that runs on Reddit's Developer Platform (Devvit).

## What the game stores
For logged-in players: your Reddit username and user ID, your guesses for each daily puzzle, your score, your streak, and your place on the daily leaderboard. For everyone: anonymous totals of how the community voted on each round.

All of it is kept in the Redis storage Reddit provides to the app. Nothing is sent to any server outside Reddit, and there are no analytics or third-party services.

## What is visible to others
Your username and score appear on the daily leaderboard of the subreddit where you play.

## Removal
Data is removed when the app is uninstalled from a subreddit. To have your entries removed sooner, open an issue at https://github.com/AradhyaTiwari10/which-sub-daily/issues

Your use of Reddit itself is covered by Reddit's own privacy policy.
